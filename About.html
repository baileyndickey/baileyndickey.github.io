About N7 Marketing.

N7 is a team of women with over 50+ years of product marketing experience. We aren’t brand or ad specialists pretending to know something about how product marketing works. We live, eat, and breathe product marketing — from product ideation, testing, and feedback to training sales teams and driving customer testimonials, we’ve done it all. We know what works and what doesn’t so you don’t have to reinvent the wheel.
